A private baby diary that lives entirely on your phone. No account, no ads, no analytics — and no internet permission at all.

<b>What you can log</b>
• Feeding — bottle (prepared vs drunk, optional bottle tracking) or timed breastfeeding with left/right sides
• Diapers — pee/poo, consistency, a stool-colour scale, rash and cream tracking
• Sleep, pumping, temperature, tummy time, baths, notes and doctor visits
• Growth — weight, height and head circumference, with WHO growth charts
• Medication courses with dose limits and "next dose due" reminders
• Solid foods with allergen tracking and reactions
• Skin conditions with daily updates and photos — export a PDF for the doctor
• Milestones and a daily photo timeline

<b>Fast to use</b>
• Live feeding and sleep timers
• Home-screen widget: log a diaper, start/stop timers, or add any entry from a pop-up menu without opening the app
• Reminders at any interval, down to the minute
• Graphs, a PDF report and a vaccination schedule

<b>Your data, your device</b>
• Backup and restore as a single .zip file, photos included
• Light, dark and true-black themes, metric or imperial units
• 20 languages, including right-to-left

<b>Not medical advice.</b> This app is a diary for your own records, not a medical device. Growth charts, temperature ranges, dose reminders and stool-colour notes are general information only. Always follow your doctor's advice.
